Mission
  • 1
    Search & parse

    Crystal Explorer takes a protein, DNA, or RNA sequence and searches the RCSB Protein Data Bank for structurally solved homologs. For every match it finds, it parses the free-text crystallization notes depositors leave behind — pH, temperature, precipitants, buffers, salts, polymers — into a structured, comparable dataset.

  • 2
    Visualize & export

    The result: interactive pH-vs-PEG and pH-vs-temperature plots colored by search score, reagent-frequency charts broken down by buffer / salt / precipitant / additive, a full per-entry conditions table with one-click 3D structure views, and exportable CSV/PDF/FAIR-metadata summaries — everything a lab needs to plan its next crystallization trial, without manually digging through dozens of PDB entries by hand.

  • 3
    Revisit anytime

    Searches run in the background, and every one is saved to your browser session's history, so past runs stay a click away instead of being rerun from scratch.

Sequence Extraction Pipeline

01. Search RCSB Database

Queries the RCSB Search API v2 with your protein, DNA, or RNA sequence — optionally tagged with your lab/institution — filtered by identity and E-value, running in the background with a live progress bar.

02. Extract Reagent Details

Mines free-text pdbx_details with a 354-reagent, 1,180+-alias dictionary, categorizing each hit into Buffer, Salt, Precipitant, or Additive and normalizing its concentration.

03. Group & Plot Data

Uses pandas and matplotlib to generate grouped-conditions CSVs, pH-vs-PEG/Temp plots, and PDF tables.

04. Generate FAIR Metadata

Converts run outputs and search parameters into DCAT-style, FAIR-compliant JSON metadata files for export.
