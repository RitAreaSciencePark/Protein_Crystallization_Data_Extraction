Mission

Crystal Explorer takes a protein, DNA, or RNA sequence and searches the RCSB Protein Data Bank for structurally solved homologs. For every match it finds, it parses the free-text crystallization notes depositors leave behind — pH, temperature, precipitants, buffers, salts, polymers — into a structured, comparable dataset.

The result: interactive pH-vs-PEG and pH-vs-temperature plots colored by sequence identity, a full per-entry conditions table with one-click 3D structure views, and exportable CSV/PDF summaries — everything a lab needs to plan its next crystallization trial, without manually digging through dozens of PDB entries by hand.

Every search is saved to a history, so past runs stay a click away instead of being rerun from scratch.

How it works

01 · Search

Find homologs

Paste a raw sequence or FASTA record. Crystal Explorer searches RCSB PDB for structures sharing your protein's fold, filtered by minimum identity and E-value.

02 · Extract

Parse conditions

Each hit's crystallization notes are mined with a chemistry-aware dictionary (with an optional LLM fallback) to pull out reagents, concentrations, pH, and temperature.

03 · Visualize

Compare & export

Browse results as interactive plots and a searchable table, jump into a 3D structure viewer, and export CSV/PDF summaries for the bench.
